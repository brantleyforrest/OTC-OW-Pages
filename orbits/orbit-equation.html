The orbit equation gives a spacecraft's distance from Earth's center at any point along its orbit. Enter values or animate one parameter at a time.

Drag to rotate the view. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
Parameters
km

Sets the size of the orbit. It is half of the orbit's longest width.

Allowed: 6,378 to 500,000. Animation: 10,000 to 60,000.

Sets the shape of the orbit. Zero is a circle, and values closer to 1 are more stretched.

Allowed: 0 to 0.99, closed orbits only. Animation: 0 to 0.7.

deg

The spacecraft's angle around the orbit, measured at Earth's center from perigee.

Allowed: 0 to 360. Animation: one full orbit, repeated.
