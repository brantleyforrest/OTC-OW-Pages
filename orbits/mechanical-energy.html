Mechanical Energy

A spacecraft trades potential energy for kinetic energy as it falls to perigee, and trades it back as it climbs to apogee. Their sum, the specific mechanical energy, never changes.

μ is Earth's gravitational parameter, 398,600 km³/s². R is measured from Earth's center: R = 6,378 km + altitude.

What to Notice
  • Toward apogee, R grows, so sPE rises toward zero and sKE falls. The spacecraft slows.
  • Toward perigee, sPE drops and sKE gains the same amount. The spacecraft speeds up.
  • ε depends only on a. It is negative for every closed orbit.
