What to Notice

Earth's equatorial bulge slowly turns a tilted orbit's plane. Just past 90° of inclination, that turn is eastward at about 1° per day, the same rate the Sun appears to move. The orbit then keeps the same local time all year.

Key Terms

RAAN (Ω): the direction of the ascending node, measured along the equator from a fixed point in space.

Node time: the local solar time on the ground below the ascending node.

Scale: the orbit is drawn larger than scale, the satellites and Earth's spin are slowed, and the Sun is at its mean position on the equator.
