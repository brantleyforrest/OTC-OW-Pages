    Key Terms
    • Target (green square): the spacecraft the RIC frame rides on. It is the origin.
    • Chase (gold triangle): the other spacecraft.
    • R: radial, away from Earth (up).
    • I: in-track, the way the target is moving (forward).
    • C: cross-track, along the orbit's angular momentum (left).
    • Rev: one orbit of the target.
    • NMC: natural motion circumnavigation, the 2-by-1 ellipse chase traces around a fixed center with no burns.
    • Drift: chase slides steadily along the track, backward when its orbit is larger, forward when smaller.
    • Drifting NMC: both at once, NMC loops that walk along the track.
    • Two different I's: blue Î is the ECI axis toward the vernal equinox; red I is in-track, the way the target is moving.
