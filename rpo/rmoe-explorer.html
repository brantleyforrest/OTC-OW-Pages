Key Terms
  • Target (green square): the origin of the RIC frame. Chase (gold triangle): the other spacecraft.
  • R radial (up), I in-track (forward), C cross-track (left).
  • NMC: natural motion circumnavigation, the 2-by-1 ellipse chase traces around a fixed center with no burns.
  • Drift: chase slides steadily along the track, backward when its orbit is larger, forward when smaller.
  • Drifting NMC: both at once, NMC loops that walk along the track.
  • Course model: the linear (CW) equations, good for a circular target orbit and a chase close to it.
  • Two different I's: blue Î is the ECI axis toward the vernal equinox; red I is in-track.
