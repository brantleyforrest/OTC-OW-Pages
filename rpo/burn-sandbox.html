    Key Terms
    • Target (green square): the origin of the RIC frame. Chase (gold triangle): the other spacecraft.
    • R radial (up), I in-track (forward), C cross-track (left).
    • Burn: an instant change in chase's velocity. Its position does not change, only where it goes next.
    • NMC: natural motion circumnavigation, the 2-by-1 ellipse chase traces with no burns. Its center is at height xC; above the target it drifts backward, below it drifts forward.
    • Natural motion: coasting with no burns. Forced motion: burning again and again to fly a path that is not natural.
    • Two different I's: blue Î is the ECI axis toward the vernal equinox; red I is in-track.
